Quick Answers

The questions we get before you even ask.

How long does shipping take?
Domestic orders ship in 1–2 business days and arrive within 3–5. International orders typically take 7–12 business days depending on customs.
What's covered under warranty?
Every STACKLY product includes a 2-year warranty against manufacturing defects. Accidental damage isn't covered, but ask us about our drop-protection replacement program.
Can I change my order after checkout?
Yes — within 1 hour of placing it. Email us with your order number and we'll update size, color, or shipping address before it ships.
Do you offer wholesale or bulk pricing?
We do, for orders of 50+ units. Select "Wholesale" in the form above and include your business details — our partnerships team replies within 2 business days.
How do returns work?
30-day no-questions-asked returns on unused items in original packaging. Start a return from your order confirmation email and we'll send a prepaid label.
